# LVL LOOL — SEASON+ MASTER STAGE PLAN

This is the design lock for Season+. It prevents random mechanic mixing.

## Global rules
- 33 stages total; all HARD; every stage 10,000+ world length.
- Stages 01–05: 12–18 planned trap groups.
- Stages 06–33: 22–25 planned trap groups.
- A single trap type is used at most twice as a named sequence in one stage.
- Every stage has one primary identity and at most two supporting mechanics.
- New mechanics are introduced in isolation before later combinations.
- Enemy, heart, gold, gem and breakable-box artwork uses the approved Season+ visual reference only.
- Pits are instant death; contact hazards use the two-hit heart system.
- Fireball kills approved enemies and breaks reward boxes.
- Box rewards are only 10 Gold, 1–3 Gems, or +1 Heart Hit.
- Reward pickups animate toward the HUD.
- No stage is generated by random mechanic selection. Layouts are hand-planned and deterministic.
